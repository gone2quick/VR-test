Smoke & Steel

Start with three seeds and a rack in a back room. Grow, sell, hire, build, and take meetings outside. Bigger deals attract ambushes and busts.

VR: point with either controller and pull the trigger. Left stick moves, right stick snap-turns. Screen: click to capture the mouse and look, WASD to move, click to interact or shoot, 1-3 hotbar, Q unequip, R reload, I inventory, Esc frees the mouse. The phone is on the table.
